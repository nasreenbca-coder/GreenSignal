About GreenSignal

Smart Alerts for a Cleaner, Plastic-Free Environment
🌱
Project Idea
GreenSignal is designed to empower citizens to take part in environmental protection by reporting plastic waste in their locality. Each report is automatically shared with collectors nearby for quick action.
🔷
Sustainability Goal
Our goal is to create a cleaner, plastic-free environment by connecting people and communities through technology — encouraging civic participation and eco-awareness.
🟣
Impact Vision
Through community collaboration, we aim to reduce unattended plastic waste, promote recycling, and encourage responsible disposal habits.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑 How We Work
For Citizens
Quickly report waste with just a tap. Our system pings nearby collectors for instant support, keeping your city plastic free and cleaner for all.
For Collectors
Register and receive real-time waste alerts matched to your city. View photo & location, act and mark as collected from your dashboard.
